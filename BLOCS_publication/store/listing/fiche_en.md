## Description complète (Apple et Google)
BLOCS is a soft, colorful liquid block puzzle you can play without pressure.

Drag pieces onto the grid, complete rows or columns to clear them, and chain combos. There is no timer: play at your own pace, for a quick break or a long evening.

WHAT MAKES BLOCS DIFFERENT
• Blocks filled with liquid that ripples as you move them
• Endless levels that keep getting harder: see how far you can go
• Rare special blocks: the bomb (3 × 3 area) and the wave (row + column)
• Piece rotations (limited per game) to get out of tricky situations
• Undo your last move
• 4 grid sizes (6×6, 7×7, 8×8, 9×9) with separate best scores
• Your own background: pick a photo from your gallery
• Spoken encouragements and sound, adjustable
• Share your scores with friends in one tap
• Works offline, no ads, no in-app purchases, no account

EASY TO LEARN
Place the pieces. Complete lines. No room left? The game ends: beat your best score!

No personal data is collected.
